recipeExcerpt: |
  A well-worn BBQ spice rub mix that I’ve been making for years—sweet, smoky, and simple enough to use on everything from chicken wings to burgers. One of the few recipes that earned a permanent spot in my tattered recipe organizer.

recipeIngredients: |
  **Hardware Needed:**
  Medium mixing bowl
  Measuring cups and spoons
  A whisk or spoon
  Airtight container (recycled spice jars work great)
  Small funnel (optional)

  **The Dry Rub:**
  1/2 cup brown sugar
  1/2 cup paprika
  1 tablespoon ground black pepper
  1 tablespoon chili powder
  1 tablespoon garlic powder
  1 tablespoon onion powder
  1 teaspoon cayenne
  1 tablespoon celery salt
  1/2 teaspoon cumin
  1/2 teaspoon ground mustard
  1/2 teaspoon coriander

recipeInstructions: |
  **Mix:** Combine all ingredients in a medium mixing bowl. Use a whisk or spoon to mix thoroughly until the sugar and spices are evenly blended. Be sure to break up any large clumps of brown sugar to ensure a consistent rub.
  **Store:** Transfer the mixture to an airtight container using a funnel if necessary. I like to keep a batch mixed up in a recycled 16oz container—it's much easier than pulling ten different jars out of the pantry every time you want to grill.
  **Use:** Coat your protein of choice generously. For best results, let the rub sit on the meat for at least 15–30 minutes before cooking to allow the flavors to penetrate.

recipeNotes: |
  **For the Wings:**
  I keep things simple. I toss the wings with olive oil, coat them generously with this spice mix, and stop right there. No marinades, no sauces, no extra steps. Spread them on a foil-lined baking pan and roast until crisp and deeply seasoned.
  **Ultimate Versatility:**
  This rub also works beautifully on burgers, pork chops, or even roasted chickpeas. It provides that signature sweet and smoky BBQ flavor without the mess of a liquid sauce.
  **Storage Tip:**
  Because of the brown sugar, this mix can occasionally clump if it sits for a long time. Simply give the jar a good shake or break up any clumps with a fork before using.
  **Yield:**
  This recipe makes about 8 oz of spice rub, which is enough for several rounds of wings or a large family cookout.

## A Go-To Blend That Never Lets Me Down ❤️

I recently shared a post called [**When Recipes Disappear**](/when-recipes-disappear) about the frustration of losing digital bookmarks and broken links. This BBQ Spice Rub is exactly the reason I started keeping a physical recipe organizer back in 2009. I don't remember exactly where I found the original formula—likely Recipezaar or an old forum—but I knew after the first time I made it that I had better write it down before it vanished like so many others.

This is one of those recipes I’ve been making long enough that it stopped feeling like a "recipe" and started feeling like a kitchen staple. It began as a quick way to season chicken wings when my kids were little and I needed a win on a busy weeknight.

It's super flavorful, uncomplicated, and put together with ingredients you likely already have in your pantry. Over time, it became the seasoning I reach for whenever I need something barbecue-adjacent—wings, chicken tenders, burgers, or whatever happens to be in the fridge that day.

## Why This Rub Works ✨

The balance of the heavy paprika and brown sugar creates a beautiful crust (bark) on the meat, while the celery salt and cumin add a savory depth that keeps it from being too "one-note." It's a formula worth passing on, documented here so it never disappears again.

- **Quick Prep:** Takes less than 5 minutes to whisk together.
- **Shelf Stable:** Stays fresh in a sealed jar for months.
- **Customizable:** Want more heat? Double the cayenne. Want it less sweet? Cut the sugar back to 1/4 cup.
