recipeExcerpt: |
  A 'miracle' dessert where spiced banana cake and creamy vanilla-rum flan switch places in the oven, topped with the theatrical sizzle of flambéed Bananas Foster.

recipeIngredients: |
  **Kitchen Tools Needed:**
  10-inch round cake pan (3" high sides) or Bundt pan
  Deep roasting pan (for water bath)
  Blender & Electric mixer
  Large skillet or flambé pan

  **For the Caramel Base:**
  1 cup dulce de leche or Mexican cajeta
  *Butter and flour for greasing the pan*

  **For the Spiced Banana Cake:**
  1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  ¾ cup unsalted butter (room temp)
  1 cup granulated sugar
  1 large egg
  ¾ cup mashed ripe bananas
  1 ½ tsp vanilla extract
  1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp cardamom, ¼ tsp nutmeg
  1 tsp baking powder, ¾ tsp baking soda, ¼ tsp salt
  1 cup buttermilk

  **For the Vanilla-Rum Flan:**
  1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  4 large eggs (room temp)
  1 tsp Mexican vanilla extract
  1 tbsp dark or spiced rum

  **For the Bananas Foster Topping:**
  4-5 ripe but firm bananas (halved lengthwise)
  ½ cup unsalted butter
  1 cup light brown sugar
  ½ tsp cinnamon
  3 oz banana liqueur
  3 oz aged rum

recipeInstructions: |
  **Prep the Canvas:** Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour your cake pan. Warm the dulce de leche slightly and pour it into the bottom of the pan to create an even base.
  **Whip Up the Cake:** Cream together butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in the egg and vanilla. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, leavening agents, and spices. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet, alternating with buttermilk. Gently fold in the mashed bananas.
  **Blend the Flan:** In a blender, combine evaporated milk, condensed milk, eggs, vanilla, and rum. Blend until perfectly smooth.
  **Assemble the Magic:** Spread the cake batter over the dulce de leche. Then, slowly and gently pour the flan mixture over the cake batter. *Note: They will look messy now, but they will switch places while baking!*
  **The Water Bath:** Place the cake pan inside a larger roasting pan. Add hot water to the roasting pan until it reaches halfway up the side of the cake pan. Bake for 55–65 minutes.
  **Cool and Chill:** Let the cake cool in the water bath for an hour, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours (overnight is best) before unmolding.
  **The Reveal:** Run a knife around the edges and invert onto a large platter. Give it a moment to release that beautiful caramel.
  **Foster the Flavor:** Just before serving, melt butter and brown sugar in a skillet with cinnamon and banana liqueur. Add bananas and cook until softened. Carefully add rum and ignite (flambé) if desired. Spoon the hot topping over the chilled cake slices.

recipeNotes: |
  **The Science:**
  This is called an 'Impossible Cake' because the dense flan mixture sinks to the bottom while the lighter cake batter rises to the top during baking, creating perfectly distinct layers.
  **Temperature Tip:**
  Ensure your eggs and butter are at room temperature to prevent the batter from curdling, which can interfere with the physical 'flip' process in the oven.
  **Safety First:**
  If you choose to flambé the topping, ensure you have a lid nearby to smother the flame if needed, and keep your face and hair away from the pan when igniting.
  **Patience is a Virtue:**
  This dessert actually gets better with time. Allowing it to chill overnight not only helps the flan set perfectly but lets the spices in the cake marry with the rum notes.

## A Dessert Miracle in the Making ❤️

Get ready to witness a dessert miracle! Today, we're jumping into a recipe that's part magic, part indulgence, and entirely unforgettable: a **Banana Cake with Flan** that flips in the oven to create a stunning banana-infused delight, all crowned with a sizzling, classic **Bananas Foster** topping.

This recipe was born from a fun challenge to see how many of my favorite sun-drenched flavors I could weave into one dessert. My inspiration pulled from two distinct culinary worlds: the decadent magic of the legendary **[Bananas Foster from Brennan's in New Orleans](https://www.brennansneworleans.com/recipes/bananas-foster/)**, and the captivating structure of a classic **Flan Imposible**. I found great technique inspiration from **[¡Pina en la Cocina!](https://pinaenlacocina.com/chocoflanthe-not-so-impossible-cake/)**.

## The Wow Factor: Why You'll Love This Cake ✨

There is something undeniably theatrical about this dessert. From the moment you invert the pan to reveal the perfectly set caramel and flan layers, to the final sizzle of the flambéed topping, it’s a showstopper. But beyond the presentation, it’s the **Flavor Symphony** that keeps people coming back. You get the deep notes from aged rum, the warmth of fresh cardamom and nutmeg, and the tropical brightness of ripe bananas in every single bite.

The best part? It’s incredibly **Make-Ahead Friendly**. Because the cake and flan *must* chill to set correctly, you can handle the heavy lifting the night before. When it's time for dessert, you're just a quick stovetop flambé away from the big reveal.
