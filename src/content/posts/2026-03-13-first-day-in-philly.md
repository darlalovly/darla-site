Took an impromptu trip to Philadelphia to see the sights and found myself impressed with so many places I discovered.

![Giant neon guitar sign outside Hard Rock Cafe in Center City Philadelphia](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-hard-rock.webp)

After a fairly quick flight from Denver, I stepped off the train in [Jefferson Station](https://www.septa.org/stations/jefferson-station) and found myself right in the middle of Center City and immediately found their [Hard Rock Cafe](https://www.hardrockcafe.com/location/philadelphia/). I've been to a few of these already so I did not visit. Fpr me, it's kinda like, if you've seen one, you've seen them all. Pretty eye-catching signage, though.

![Contrafuerte aluminum sculpture by Miguel Antonio Horn spanning across an alleyway bridge on Cuthbert Street](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-contrafuerte.webp)

Next, I found this super interesting sculpture of intertwining bodies that appear to be holding up a pedestrian bridge between a couple buildings. After the trip, I discovered from a social media post that this particular piece is entitled, [*Contrafuerte*](https://www.miguelantoniohorn.com/portfolio/contrafuerte) and was created by Philadelphia artist named [Miguel Antonio Horn](https://www.miguelantoniohorn.com/) in 2021.

![Middle Eastern falafel and fresh juice stand with red counter stools inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-falafel.webp)

![Giunta's Prime Shop meat and butcher display inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-giuntas.webp)

![Philbert the Pig bronze piggy bank sculpture inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-philbert.webp)

Now, I heard how great [Reading Terminal Market](https://readingterminalmarket.org/) was and while I did find the place interesting with lots of variety, I also thought most of it was overpriced. Especially after checking out a few other spots around the city. Philly has some good food, y'all. I just found better prices in better places than Reading Terminal Market. Maybe if I lived in Center City I'd find it useful for unique ingredients. However, as a tourist, well...it was pretty, but pricey.

![Glass bakery display filled with handcrafted truffles and chocolates at Mueller Chocolate Co in Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-candy.webp)

![Godshall's Poultry counter with stacks of fresh eggs inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-goodshalls.webp)

![Halteman Family Meats butcher display counter inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-halteman.webp)

![Fresh green asparagus on display in front of Iovine's Produce neon sign at Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-iovine-produce.webp)

![Vibrant floral Water Gives Life mural by Eurhi Jones and David McShane on the side of a brick building](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-water-gives-life.webp)

They have some amazing murals around Philly and I fully intended to do the entire self-guided walking tour over on [Mural Arts Philadelphia](https://muralarts.org/tours/self-guided/) but, today I only caught up with one, [*Water Gives Life*](https://www.muralarts.org/artworks/water-gives-life/) by Eurhi Jones and David McShane. Too bad it had this chain-link fence blocking my camera lense.

![Full view of the historic Philadelphia City Hall tower topped with the William Penn statue](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-city-hall.webp)

Of course, I had to snap a pic of William Penn atop their [historic City Hall](https://www.phila.gov/departments/city-representative/philadelphia-city-hall/).

![Exterior storefront of Foods on First diner decorated for St. Patrick's Day](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first.webp)

Then I had to find something to eat and stopped in at [Foods on First](https://www.foodsonfirstdiner.com/) for a Reuben and some hot tea. Since it was getting close to St. Patrick's Day, the place was all dolled up in sparkly green decor, leprechauns, and clovers. That Reuben was really friggin' good and man, lemme tell ya, food in Philly was so delicious and inexpensive compared to Colorado! That was one thing I just could not get over.

![Fresh Reuben sandwich served with hot tea at Foods on First](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first-rueban.webp)

After that I had to head to the [Hilton Garden Inn](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/phlcdgi-hilton-garden-inn-philadelphia-center-city/) for some downtime and to rest up for the next leg of my visit, Philly's own [Chinatown](/posts/2026-03-13-philly-chinatown).
