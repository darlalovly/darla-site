Took an impromptu trip to Philadelphia to see the sights and found myself impressed with so many places I discovered.

![Giant neon guitar sign outside Hard Rock Cafe in Center City Philadelphia](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-hard-rock.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

After a fairly quick flight from Denver, I stepped off the train in [Jefferson Station](https://www.septa.org/stations/jefferson-station) and found myself right in the middle of Center City and immediately found their [Hard Rock Cafe](https://www.hardrockcafe.com/location/philadelphia/).[cite: 14, 15] I've been to a few of these already so I did not visit.[cite: 14, 15] Fpr me, it's kinda like, if you've seen one, you've seen them all.[cite: 14, 15] Pretty eye-catching signage, though.[cite: 14, 15]

![Contrafuerte aluminum sculpture by Miguel Antonio Horn spanning across an alleyway bridge on Cuthbert Street](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-contrafuerte.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

Next, I found this super interesting sculpture of intertwining bodies that appear to be holding up a pedestrian bridge between a couple buildings.[cite: 14, 15] After the trip, I discovered from a social media post that this particular piece is entitled, [*Contrafuerte*](https://www.miguelantoniohorn.com/portfolio/contrafuerte) and was created by Philadelphia artist named [Miguel Antonio Horn](https://www.miguelantoniohorn.com/) in 2021.[cite: 14, 15]

![Middle Eastern falafel and fresh juice stand with red counter stools inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-falafel.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Giunta's Prime Shop meat and butcher display inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-giuntas.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Philbert the Pig bronze piggy bank sculpture inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-philbert.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

Now, I heard how great [Reading Terminal Market](https://readingterminalmarket.org/) was and while I did find the place interesting with lots of variety, I also thought most of it was overpriced.[cite: 14, 15] Especially after checking out a few other spots around the city.[cite: 14, 15] Philly has some good food, y'all.[cite: 14, 15] I just found better prices in better places than Reading Terminal Market.[cite: 14, 15] Maybe if I lived in Center City I'd find it useful for unique ingredients.[cite: 14, 15] However, as a tourist, well...it was pretty, but pricey.[cite: 14, 15]

![Glass bakery display filled with handcrafted truffles and chocolates at Mueller Chocolate Co in Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-candy.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Godshall's Poultry counter with stacks of fresh eggs inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-goodshalls.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Halteman Family Meats butcher display counter inside Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-halteman.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Fresh green asparagus on display in front of Iovine's Produce neon sign at Reading Terminal Market](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-reading-terminal-iovine-produce.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

![Vibrant floral Water Gives Life mural by Eurhi Jones and David McShane on the side of a brick building](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-water-gives-life.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

They have some amazing murals around Philly and I fully intended to do the entire self-guided walking tour over on [Mural Arts Philadelphia](https://muralarts.org/tours/self-guided/) but, today I only caught up with one, [*Water Gives Life*](https://www.muralarts.org/artworks/water-gives-life/) by Eurhi Jones and David McShane.[cite: 14, 15] Too bad it had this chain-link fence blocking my camera lense.[cite: 14, 15]

![Full view of the historic Philadelphia City Hall tower topped with the William Penn statue](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-city-hall.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

Of course, I had to snap a pic of William Penn atop their [historic City Hall](https://www.phila.gov/departments/city-representative/philadelphia-city-hall/).[cite: 14, 15]

![Exterior storefront of Foods on First diner decorated for St. Patrick's Day](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

Then I had to find something to eat and stopped in at [Foods on First](https://www.foodsonfirstdiner.com/) for a Reuben and some hot tea.[cite: 14, 15] Since it was getting close to St. Patrick's Day, the place was all dolled up in sparkly green decor, leprechauns, and clovers.[cite: 14, 15] That Reuben was really friggin' good and man, lemme tell ya, food in Philly was so delicious and inexpensive compared to Colorado![cite: 14, 15] That was one thing I just could not get over.[cite: 14, 15]

![Fresh Reuben sandwich served with hot tea at Foods on First](/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first-rueban.webp)[cite: 14, 15]

After that I had to head to the [Hilton Garden Inn](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/phlcdgi-hilton-garden-inn-philadelphia-center-city/) for some downtime and to rest up for the next leg of my visit, Philly's own [Chinatown](/posts/2026-03-13-philly-chinatown).[cite: 14, 15]
