Spent my first night in Philly wandering around their Chinatown and found it a remarkable neighborhood. The streets were bustling with activity, a few robots were making deliveries, and there was so much good food to be found in the area.

There are a number of local markets, delis, fruit stands, bakeries, bubble tea, smoothies, and a bevy of noodle shops alongside options for Korean and Japanese, as well. You'll find specialty stores with every little trinket under the sun and everything Hello Kitty! There were so many interesting murals, unique architecture, and the locals were super friendly. I adore this neighborhood.

I kinda went crazy over QQ Mart. Their seafood salad was so friggin' good. They had Ice Water and [Day's Soda](https://daysbeverages.com/), Champagne Cola was an easy fave! It was super close to the [Hilton Garden Inn](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/phlctgi-hilton-garden-inn-philadelphia-center-city/) and seemed to always be open when I needed something quick.

The thing I really admired the most about Philly's Chinatown was their sense of community. In 2026, I found it very refreshing the way they recognized the various immigrants who make this neighborhood their home. I could have spent the entire span of my visit to Philly in these few blocks. There were so many places I wanted to visit and just didn't get the chance. I've got so many places in mind for my next trip.
