  <p>Attended the final Monster Day in Greeley. This has always been a fun event my family looked forward to every summer. There's always fabulous costumes, music, unique vendors, local crafts, and spooky cars[cite: 31]. Sadly, this seems to be the last year the event will be held in downtown Greeley[cite: 31].</p>
  <p>Monster Day began as a collaboration with <a href="https://distortionsunlimited.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Distortions Unlimited</a>, Visit Greeley, and the Downtown Development Authority to benefit the "Don't Be A Monster" anti-bullying campaign.</p>
