Went to the Red River Revel to hear some bands and look at some art in downtown Shreveport's Festival Plaza. The 2026 double-weekend event started on Thursday, October 8th and will wrap up on Sunday, October 18th. This was my first time in attendance and the festival's 50th Anniversary. The grounds open at 11am and close down at 11pm.

I got lucky with a parking spot a couple blocks away and followed the sounds of the music until I found the entrance.

<img src="/images/posts/2026-10-15-red-river-revel/red-river-revel-vendors.webp" alt="Art and food vendors at Festival Plaza" class="float-right ml-6 mb-4 w-full sm:w-1/2 md:w-5/12 rounded-none" style="float: right; margin: 0.25rem 0 1.25rem 1.5rem; max-width: 48%; height: auto; clear: right;" />

While I've heard quite a bit about the artists and musicians appearing and performing at the event, I wasn't quite sure what to expect. There must've been around 100 different vendors ranging from chefs serving up local favorites to regional artists displaying and selling their work while a regular schedule of musicians and djs performed across the plaza.

<img src="/images/posts/2026-10-15-red-river-revel/red-river-revel-jw1.webp" alt="Jimmy Wooten performing live on stage" class="float-left mr-6 mb-4 w-full sm:w-1/2 md:w-5/12 rounded-none" style="float: left; margin: 0.25rem 1.5rem 1.25rem 0; max-width: 48%; height: auto; clear: left;" />

When I arrived, Jimmy Wooten was on the main stage performing. There were plenty of tables and chairs under the awnings of the stages in case you want to just sit and listen, take a break, or enjoy a bite to eat.

<img src="/images/posts/2026-10-15-red-river-revel/red-river-revel-bte1.webp" alt="Better Than Ezra headlining at the Red River Revel" class="float-right ml-6 mb-4 w-full sm:w-1/2 md:w-5/12 rounded-none" style="float: right; margin: 0.25rem 0 1.25rem 1.5rem; max-width: 48%; height: auto; clear: right;" />

The evening headliner was Better Than Ezra who started off with *King of New Orleans*. Near the stage was packed with people dancing and singing along. It was just too hot and muggy for me to remain in the middle of all those bodies so I gave up my view somewhere around *I Wanna Be Sedated*.

As an aside, I realize it's October but I certainly wish whoever was sporting that BR540 (or some relative dupe) had kept themselves on the sidelines and closer to a breeze. BR540 does not smell good wafting between that many sweaty people. It would not have been my choice of scent for an outdoor event in this weather.

<img src="/images/posts/2026-10-15-red-river-revel/red-river-revel-ccbo.webp" alt="Cody Cooke and the Bayou Outlaws performing" class="float-left mr-6 mb-4 w-full sm:w-1/2 md:w-5/12 rounded-none" style="float: left; margin: 0.25rem 1.5rem 1.25rem 0; max-width: 48%; height: auto; clear: left;" />

Once I got away from the big crowd, I ventured toward the more subdued crowd listening to the country sounds of Cody Cooke and the Bayou Outlaws.

Anyway, the event was lively, not as expensive as I anticipated, and there was something there for everybody. I will definitely go back. Maybe even one more time this weekend.
