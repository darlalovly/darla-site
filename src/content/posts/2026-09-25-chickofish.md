    <p>I have a new fast-food favorite in Shreveport - the fish plate at Chick o'Fish on Mansfield! You know how you gotta have that fish on Fridays? I love how they fry up these thin filets! This place is my new go-to for fried catfish. Their tartar sauce is not white, y'all! And it is so, so good, you're gonna wanna ask for extra. Their red beans & rice ain't half-bad, either.</p>
    <p>I just tried this place on a whim. Nobody ever told me about it but I am telling you. When you're cravin' catfish in a hurry, and you find yourself in Shreveport, this is the place to get it.</p>
