Life is constantly changing, and sometimes those changes come hard and fast. I suppose that is what happened with me earlier in 2026. It has taken me some time to return to a place where I can share what I am thinking, and it seems that time is now.

This space will be an outlet for my current thoughts and musings on whatever crosses my mind going forward.

Currently on somewhat of a nomadic journey these days, finding my next place in this world. While nothing much of me has changed, everything is constantly changing around me.

The sky is dark and clear. The night air smells fresh. I smell like Egyptian Musk.
