    <p>Today, I stopped into Meeker's Colorado Kitchen & Bar in Greeley for brunch. This is a spot I frequent that regularly updates their menu. I am a big fan of Eggs Benedict and today I enjoyed Meeker's Southwest Benedict made with Poached Eggs, I chose a slice of ham to have on the toasted English Muffin smothered in Pork Green Chili. This was an excellent version of one of my brunch favorites.</p>
    <p>It came with a side of their Skillet Potatoes. Now, I love how they prepare and serve their Skillet Potatoes and often select them for breakfast but today when I had their Pork Green Chili, I had to ask for an extra cup for those potatoes, too! I will start asking for my Skillet Potatoes loaded. I think Meeker's is one of the best kept secrets in Greeley. The food and atmosphere is always enjoyable!</p>
