<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>Today I got to check out the Bonnie and Clyde Ambush Museum in Gibsland, Louisiana. There is an extensive collection of artifacts including the car Bonnie and Clyde were travelling in when they were killed May 23, 1934. Clyde was known for being an accurate gunman so law enforcement didn't give them a chance to fire. They just unloaded their weapons on the car and riddled the couple's bodies with bullets.</p>
