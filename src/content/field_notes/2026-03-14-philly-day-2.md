<!-- SECTION 1: PHILLY'S STEAK & PIZZA -->
<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 sm:gap-6 mb-6 sm:mb-8 items-start w-full">
  <div class="flex flex-col space-y-3">
    <h2 class="text-sm sm:text-base font-mono uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-neutral-300 m-0">Philly's Steak & Pizza</h2>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">Didn't get started until around noon. So for a quick lunch, I walked across the street to <a href="https://www.phillyssteakandpizza.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-white transition-colors">Philly's Steak & Pizza</a> for a classic Cheesesteak. No way I was spending time in Philly without a cheesesteak. The bread was soft and hugged all the ingredients without getting soggy.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">For $15, I found it to be an amazing deal. As mentioned in a <a href="/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-white transition-colors">previous post</a>, the prices in Philly were shockingly cheap compared to the prices I'm accustomed to paying in Colorado.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">Everything was prepared fresh while I watched. Philly's Steak & Pizza is a family business. Daddy took the orders and ran the register while the son prepared the sandwiches. They were so friendly and the place was so convenient to my hotel. No doubt I'll return to try some pizza before I leave this city.</p>
    <p class="m-0 font-mono text-xs text-neutral-400 pt-1">📍 1100 Market St, Philadelphia, PA 19107</p>

<!-- SECTION 2: MR. WISH -->
<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 sm:gap-6 mb-6 sm:mb-8 items-start w-full">

  <div class="flex flex-col space-y-3 order-1 md:order-2">
    <h2 class="text-sm sm:text-base font-mono uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-neutral-300 m-0">Mr. Wish</h2>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">Next, it was a quick stop into <a href="https://www.mrwishusa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-white transition-colors">Mr. Wish</a>. I noticed so many spots for coffee and tea in Chinatown last night, I knew I'd be back in the morning for something to sip.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">This place had an incredible variety of fresh teas and smoothies available. I kinda wanted something hot but they way they were blending everything up behind the counter, I had to try something new to me.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">I decided on an Iced Red Bean & Sago Coconut Milk. While it was delicious, I could only finish about a third. Not sure if there's too much caffeine for my system to handle or some other ingredient that just made the drink unagreeable for me. It tasted good but gave me heartburn. I have been super sensitive to caffeine lately.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">Next time, I'll go for something Mango.</p>
    <p class="m-0 font-mono text-xs text-neutral-400 pt-1">📍 1102 Chestnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19107</p>
  </div>
</div>

    <h2 class="text-sm sm:text-base font-mono uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-neutral-300 m-0">Elfreth's Alley</h2>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">I finally arrived at <a href="https://www.elfrethsalley.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-white transition-colors">Elfreth's Alley</a> but felt a bit guilty for wandering so close to private entrances. This row of historic homes were built between 1720 and 1836. The local architecture of Philly just seem so practical to me, even in this day and age.</p>
    <p class="m-0 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">If you want to see how the interior of these places are designed, you can visit the Windsor Chair Maker's Home which is operated as the <a href="https://www.elfrethsalley.org/museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-white transition-colors">Elfreth's Alley Museum</a>. Or, you can always go check for real estate listings to view interior shots of the current residences for sale.</p>
    <p class="m-0 font-mono text-xs text-neutral-400 pt-1">📍 126 Elfreth's Alley, Philadelphia, PA 19106</p>
