<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Stopped into the Turkish Cafe Co. for a quick lunch on Saturday. This was a delightful little spot I anticiaptated visiting since first learning of their opening. I am a sucker for pretty food and this place did not disappoint on the aesthitics, at all.</p>
    <p>The interior was quiet with a nice area on the upper level for lounging with a friend for deep conversation over a hot coffee or turkish tea. It might be a spot I'd frequent if I needed wifi and a quiet place to think.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-09-12-turkish-cafe-co/turkish-cafe-co-interior.webp" alt="Turkish Cafe Co interior dining and upper lounge area" class="w-full h-full object-cover" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>I enjoyed a cup of turkish tea, some Hummus, and Lamb Lahmujan. The hummus had a nice, very smooth consistency and served with crispy triangles of warm pita. The lahmujan is simply minced meat lightly seasoned and spread on a thin soft crust. I was expecting it to be more like Safeiha I've eaten in Lebanese restaurnats in the past, however, the dough of this lahmujan was much thinner and easier to garnish and roll like a wrap.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-09-12-turkish-cafe-co/turkish-cafe-co-ricotta-pistachio-cake.webp" alt="Slice of Ricotta Pistachio Cake at Turkish Cafe Co" class="w-full h-full object-cover" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Now, I must admit it was the beautiful desserts that lured me to this cafe. The cases are filled with beautiful and no doubt delicious sweet treats sure to satisfy your cravings. To finish, I had a slice of Ricotta Pistachio Cake. The three-layer delight is made in-house and features a layer of pistachio mousse, a lightly sweetended layer of whipped ricotta cream, topped with a thin layer of sponge delicately dusted with powdered sugar, and rimmed with a coating of crunchy pistachios. It was lightly sweet, creamy, and the just the perfect ending to an easy lunch.</p>
  </div>
</div>

The prices were more than reasonable for delicacies prepared with love in this local kitchen. You can visit the <a href="https://turkishcafeco.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4">Turkish Cafe Co online</a> to drool over their offerings.</p>
