<div class="mb-8 space-y-4">
  <p>I finally made time to get a closer look at Mount Rushmore. The natural formations of rocks and forest in the Black Hills is absolutely, breathtakingly beautiful. The drive up to Mount Rushmore National Memorial offers you so much to see and do. There's lots of local attractions along the way. You really need several days if you have a desire to truly know and understand the area. The town of Keystone is one of the most picturesque locations I've ever had a chance to witness. It's the one place I wish I had more time to explore.</p>
  <p>At the time of my visit, early morning, the deer trotting along the edge of the highway seemed to be in abundance. It was an amazing view all along the way. I really wanted to catch the Memorial at sunrise with only a very few visitors wandering the grounds. Parking is only $10 with a return receipt good for a full year.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-4 order-2 md:order-1">
    <p>Evidently the sculptor, Gutzon Borglum, chose the location for its massive size and granite composition. While the huge carvings of the presidents are impressive, it's the vastness of the overall landscape alone that I find most inspiring. You really don't need to see four dudes carved into the side of the mountain to enjoy the area.</p>
    <p>George Washington, our very first president, was selected to symbolize the birth of the nation, laying the foundation for American democracy.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-square w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800 order-1 md:order-2">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-10-02-mount-rushmore-up-close/2026-10-02-mount-rushmore-gutzon-borglum.webp" alt="Bronze bust of Mount Rushmore sculptor Gutzon Borglum on a granite pedestal" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mb-8 space-y-4">
  <p>Thomas Jefferson, was included for his role in authoring the Declaration of Independence and also signified expansion of the country after signing the Louisiana Purchase in 1803, which effectively doubled the size of the United States.</p>
  <p>Theodore Roosevelt is the most modern president included and represents the development of the country and establishing global influence and economic maturity. He and the sculptor were apparently close friends and political allies.</p>
  <p>Abraham Lincoln was selected to represent the preservation of the United States after guiding the country through the Civil War. Lincoln was the subject for the sculptor in two other major works, The Seated Lincoln located in Newark, New Jersey and the large marble bust of Lincoln in the US Capitol Crypt. Borglum even named his son Lincoln.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-10-02-mount-rushmore-up-close/2026-10-02-mount-rushmore-profile.webp" alt="Side profile of George Washington carved into the granite rock face viewed from the winding highway" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>There's a certain sense of calm to be found in venturing the roads through the Black Hills. The area is considered sacred by the Lakota Sioux. It's easy to understand the controversy surrounding Mount Rushmore, formerly known by the Lakota as Six Grandfathers. The land was ceded to the tribe in the Treaty of Fort Laramie in 1868 but illegally seized by the US Government after gold was discovered in the area in the 1870s. For this reason, Native Americans often view the monument as a desecration of their land rather than some great national symbol.</p>
    <p>Upon exiting the Memorial grounds the profile of George Washington is clearly visible. This may be my favorite view of all.</p>
  </div>
