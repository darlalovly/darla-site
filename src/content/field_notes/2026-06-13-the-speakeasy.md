<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Had drinks and dessert at The Speakeasy tonight. This is a cozy little dark spot you'll find somewhat hidden underneath The Kress Cinema. The Kress is my absolute favorite spot to catch a movie in downtown Greeley and I have been dying to get a chance to visit their little prohibition-style bar. The theatre always serves the best drinks (the only place I want to drink pina coladas) so I knew this visit would be an absolute treat!</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">

  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-06-13-the-speakeasy/drink-menu.webp" alt="Sours section of The Speakeasy cocktail menu listing The Last Word cocktail" class="w-full h-full object-cover" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>I ordered The Last Word (Gin, Green Chartreuse, Luxardo Maraschino, and Lime) and let's just say, these are not drinks you want to gulp. Despite being super-strong and tart, every sip was delicious. Exactly the kind of cocktails I enjoy!</p>
    <p>Seems they change their cocktail menu regularly, so you may not catch this one on the menu when you visit, but I can't imagine you wouldn't enjoy anything they pour.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Paired it with their flourless chocolate torte, which was rich, velvety smooth, and an absolute perfect end to a great night out.</p>
    <p>10/10 for date-night or any time a late-evening cocktail is calling! If you haven't had a chance to check this place out, definitely add it to your list of unique spots in northern Colorado.</p>
