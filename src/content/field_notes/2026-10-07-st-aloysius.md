    <p>As many times as I've travelled up and down I-25 through Colorado, I can't believe I never caught a glimpse of the old ruins of St. Aloysius. But on a recent trip north, I spotted the cross just between the trees. On my way back south, I decided to get a closer look and snap a picture.</p>
    <p>I really had no idea what the site was, but a brief Google search explained this was the only remaining ruins of what's now a ghost town. The town was known as Morley and was known as a mining town that supplied coal to the steel mill in Pueblo. The place was eventually demolished in the 1950s. Well, all except for what still stands of St. Aloysius. Evidently, the demolition crew considered it sacrilegious to knock down the old church and left it standing.</p>
    <p>I hoped to get a closer look but the cameras and warnings encouraged me to turn around as I have no time to deal with a trespassing charge. I snapped the pic just off I-25 going south between exits 2 and 1. If you're travelling I-25 and blink, you just might miss it.</p>
