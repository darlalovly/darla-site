<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>Took an impromptu trip to Philadelphia to see the sights and found myself impressed with so many places I discovered.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>After a fairly quick flight from Denver, I stepped off the train in <a href="https://www.septa.org/stations/jefferson-station" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Jefferson Station</a> and found myself right in the middle of Center City and immediately found their <a href="https://www.hardrockcafe.com/location/philadelphia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Hard Rock Cafe</a>. I've been to a few of these already so I did not visit. Fpr me, it's kinda like, if you've seen one, you've seen them all. Pretty eye-catching signage, though.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-hard-rock.webp" alt="Giant neon guitar sign outside Hard Rock Cafe in Center City Philadelphia" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-contrafuerte.webp" alt="Contrafuerte aluminum sculpture by Miguel Antonio Horn spanning across an alleyway bridge on Cuthbert Street" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Next, I found this super interesting sculpture of intertwining bodies that appear to be holding up a pedestrian bridge between a couple buildings. After the trip, I discovered from a social media post that this particular piece is entitled, <em>Contrafuerte</em> and was created by Philadelphia artist named <a href="https://www.miguelantoniohorn.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Miguel Antonio Horn</a> in 2021.</p>
  </div>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mb-3 space-y-3">
  <p>Now, I heard how great <a href="https://readingterminalmarket.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Reading Terminal Market</a> was and while I did find the place interesting with lots of variety, I also thought most of it was overpriced. Especially after checking out a few other spots around the city. Philly has some good food, y'all. I just found better prices in better places than Reading Terminal Market. Maybe if I lived in Center City I'd find it useful for unique ingredients. However, as a tourist, well...it was pretty, but pricey.</p>
</div>

  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>They have some amazing murals around Philly and I fully intended to do the entire self-guided walking tour over on <a href="https://muralarts.org/tours/self-guided/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Mural Arts Philadelphia</a> but, today I only caught up with one, <em>Water Gives Life</em> by Eurhi Jones and David McShane. Too bad it had this chain-link fence blocking my camera lense.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[4/3] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-water-gives-life.webp" alt="Vibrant floral Water Gives Life mural by Eurhi Jones and David McShane on the side of a brick building" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly-city-hall.webp" alt="Full view of the historic Philadelphia City Hall tower topped with the William Penn statue" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Of course, I had to snap a pic of William Penn atop their historic City Hall.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-start">
  <div class="flex flex-col space-y-4">
    <p>Then I had to find something to eat and stopped in at Foods on First for a Reuben and some hot tea. Since it was getting close to St. Patrick's Day, the place was all dolled up in sparkly green decor, leprechauns, and clovers. That Reuben was really friggin' good and man, lemme tell ya, food in Philly was so delicious and inexpensive compared to Colorado! That was one thing I just could not get over.</p>
    <div class="aspect-[4/3] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
      <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first-rueban.webp" alt="Fresh Reuben sandwich served with hot tea at Foods on First" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-03-13-first-day-in-philly/2026-03-13-food-on-first.webp" alt="Exterior storefront of Foods on First diner decorated for St. Patrick's Day" class="w-full h-full object-cover rounded-xl" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>After that I had to head to the Hilton Garden Inn for some downtime and to rest up for the next leg of my visit, Philly's own Chinatown.</p>
</div>
