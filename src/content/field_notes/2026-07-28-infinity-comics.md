Got to spend some time catching up with my old friend, Wayne, today! If you haven't checked out Infinity Comics in Antique Alley, get over there and see all the great collectibles in stock—especially if you're a comic book fan. Wayne has built up a massive, incredible collection that covers everything from vintage back issues and rare grail finds to current releases and pop culture memorabilia. You could easily spend hours digging through the stacks and finding something special.

Whether you're looking to hunt down a key issue, add to your collection, or just talk comics with Wayne, definitely make a stop next time you find yourself in downtown West Monroe!
