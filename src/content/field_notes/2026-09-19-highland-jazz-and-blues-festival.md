<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>At the last minute, I learned the <a href="https://www.highlandjazzandblues.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-neutral-600">Highland Jazz & Blues Festival</a> was happening and Ivan Neville's Dumpstaphunk would be performing. Their set was conducted on the Buddy Flett stage and sponsored by Live Casino. This was the second year of the festival and even if I was late to hear of the event, I wasn't gonna let that cause me to miss this New Orleans Funk band brought into existence by a couple of the Neville brothers, Ivan and Charles.</p>
  <p>You know, there ain't much better than listening to some Louisiana legends perform on a Saturday afternoon in the park. The smell of patchouli wafted through the air while all ages and colors danced with Dumpstaphunk performing songs such as, <em>I Wish You Would</em>, <em>Justice</em>, <em>Do You</em>, and <em>Deeper</em>.</p>
</div>
