<div class="mb-8 space-y-3">
  <p>During a recent visit to Philadelphia, I took the train from Philly to Newark and into NYC to catch a few iconic spots.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>Now, I know most people think of NYC and pizza but I had to get a hot dog and papaya smoothie from the legendary Gray's Papaya. This will forever be a spot I visit whenever I'm in the city. While I haven't had my mouth on one of these hot dogs since the 90s, it was just as I remembered - simple, cheap, and delicious. I am not an onion lover but that sauce has my heart. And while I love their Pina Colada, I had to go with the papaya smoothie as it is such a nice compliment and kinda makes me feel like I'm consuming something healthy next to those hot dogs!</p>
  </div>
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">

  </div>
</div>

<div class="mb-8 overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
  <img src="/images/posts/2026-05-22-nyc/nyc-grays-papaya.webp" alt="Wide view of Gray's Papaya building at Amsterdam Ave and 72nd Street" class="w-full h-auto object-cover" />
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 items-center">
  <div class="aspect-[3/4] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 shadow-sm dark:border-neutral-800">
    <img src="/images/posts/2026-05-22-nyc/nyc-statue-of-liberty.webp" alt="Full view of the Statue of Liberty from the water aboard the Staten Island Ferry" class="w-full h-full object-cover" />
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-center space-y-3">
    <p>I hadn't seen Lady Liberty in many years and this time I caught the view from the Staten Island Ferry. She's still just as iconic as I remember and the breeze off the water was a nice reprieve. In past visits, I've been to the island and I have to say she never seemed as big up close as she did from the water. Viewing the Statue of Liberty I had to reflect on how leaders in France must've felt offering such a substantial gift to the United States then and now. In 2026, I sincerely hope they don't regret their gift.</p>
    <p>So, whenever you visit NYC don't hesitate to think hot dog rather than pizza. And for sure, catch a glimpse of Lady Liberty while she still stands.</p>
  </div>
</div>
